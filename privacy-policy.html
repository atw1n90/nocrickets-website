1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you create an account, use our services, or contact us. This includes:

  • Account information: Name, email address, business name, phone number, and billing address.
  • Payment information: Processed securely through Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data: How you interact with the NoCrickets platform, including chatbot configurations, booking activity, and integration settings.
  • Communications: Messages you send to us through support channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the NoCrickets platform.
  • Process payments and manage your subscription.
  • Send transactional emails and account notifications.
  • Respond to support requests and customer inquiries.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We use artificial intelligence provided by Anthropic to power chatbot and lead qualification features within the platform. AI-generated outputs are facilitated through your configured workspace settings.

3. Information Shared with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted service providers necessary to operate our platform:

  • Stripe — Payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — AI chatbot and automation features.
  • Easy!Appointments / Google Calendar — Booking and calendar integration.
  • DigitalOcean — Cloud infrastructure hosting.

All third-party providers are required to handle your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

4. Data Processed on Behalf of Clients

This section is important. Please read it carefully.

NoCrickets provides automation tools that our clients use to capture and manage leads and appointment data from their own customers and prospects. In these cases, Caelum Group Ventures LLC acts as a data processor, processing data on behalf of our clients, who are the data controllers.

Our clients are solely responsible for:

  • Ensuring they have obtained appropriate consent from their own customers to collect and process their data through the NoCrickets platform.
  • Complying with all applicable privacy laws in their jurisdiction regarding the data they collect.
  • Providing their own customers with appropriate privacy disclosures.

NoCrickets does not sell, share, or use end-user lead data collected through client workspaces for any purpose other than delivering the contracted services to that client.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. If you cancel your account, your data will be retained for a reasonable period to allow for account recovery or dispute resolution, after which it will be deleted in accordance with our internal data management policies.

Lead and appointment data processed on behalf of clients is retained in accordance with the client's workspace settings and our service agreement.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to operate the platform, including session authentication and user preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers.

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect platform functionality.
